Approved equipment comparison redesign

References: approved-five-stats.png and approved-one-stat.png (user-approved concept images).
Prefab: Assets/DarkFairytale/UI/Equipment/Prefabs/StorybookEquipment.prefab.

Quality and title occupy one single line, with bounded font fitting and ellipsis for long titles.
Equipment artwork is dynamically bound to the current/target item sprites behind stat rows. The translucent parchment veil and image are separate editable prefab nodes. Existing game equipment assets and frame/button assets are reused; no generated text or icons are baked into the runtime UI.
ReplacementDivider is a persistent UGUI graphic: the continuous vertical contour has a right-facing notch. Thickness, notch size, offset and colors are Inspector-editable. It stretches with panel height without stretching a raster texture.
The comparison uses 303 reference units for five rows and a minimum of 214 for one row, preserving the action column. Spare vertical space centers the attribute rows. Primary stat uses bold, secondary stats use normal dark text. Red/green delta conventions remain unchanged.

Unity validation images: output/equipment-layered-comparison/; six full-screen captures, 1 and 5 stat fixtures at 1080x1920, 768x1024, 720x1440. Test fixtures do not save to player account.
Visual implementation follows the approved arrangement. Runtime uses the actual inventory club sprite (simpler than the concept rendering) and the project's existing font/frame/button artwork; long titles may elide at narrow widths, so this is not a claim of pixel-identical concept reproduction.
