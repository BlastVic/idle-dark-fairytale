Use case: ui-mockup
Create a polished design presentation for a portrait mobile dark fairytale idle RPG FUSION interface. Two equally sized complete 9:16 portrait UI screens side by side, on a neutral charcoal background, high-resolution landscape board. Small external headings above screens: “融合 · 喂养神像” and “融合 · 等待提升”.
Input reference 1: approved shop UI STYLE reference only. Match its antique ivory parchment, delicate black thorn ink borders with restrained worn gold stitching, deep crimson wax and leather buttons, readable black Chinese serif type, engraved storybook illustration. Do not copy shop content.
Input reference 2: existing idol SUBJECT identity reference. Preserve its recognizable squat block stone idol, wide rectangular grin, two large rounded eyes and short pointed ears/horns. Reinterpret its surface in weathered grey stone with restrained desaturated blue mineral details and moss, hand-engraved storybook treatment. No new humanoid deity, no skull replacement, no bright cartoon orange. This is a proposed art adaptation of the existing idol.
Both pages have the SAME stable layout:
1. Compact top header, crimson back ribbon left, central text “融合”, forest silhouette ornaments, small subtitle “庇护所 · 古老神像”. No player avatar needed.
2. A slim wallet row with small ordinary BLUE crystal icon and label “持有水晶”, count. Blue crystals are a DIFFERENT ordinary currency from the red Blood Moon currency. No red crystal icon anywhere.
3. Main shrine illustration taking about 35% height, identifiable stone idol on low altar in a thorny woodland niche, parchment background, subtle blue crystals offered in a shallow bowl, a few floating blue motes. Keep illustration framed naturally by branches, no huge opaque landscape rectangle. Same idol in both screens. Right state slightly illuminated blue eyes and softly glowing bowl, no excessive VFX.
4. Centered clear progression “融合等级  Lv.6 → Lv.7”. Small label “融合进度”. Horizontal thick antique metal framed BLUE progress bar with numeric text legible.
5. Compact parchment inset titled “本次提升奖励”, two reward entries side by side with restrained ink icons: “属性点 +1” and “经验 +50”. No equipment crafting, no item slots, no random reward chest, no guaranteed skill point here.
6. Bottom action area, breathing space, strong crimson embossed large button, one supporting line. Bottom small text button “返回庇护所”. No five-tab nav because this is a focused shelter subpage. No bottom screen clipping.
LEFT state: wallet “1,280”. Progress bar filled exactly about 58%, text “180 / 310”. Under rewards clear spend row BLUE crystal icon “补满消耗 130 水晶”. Main button “喂养神像”. Supporting line “消耗水晶补满融合进度”.
RIGHT state: wallet “1,150”. Progress bar full, text “310 / 310”. Same rewards panel. Spend row replaced by wax seal check and text “进度已满 · 可以提升”. Main button “提升融合等级”. Supporting line “提升后领取本次奖励”.
Readable exact simplified Chinese. Clear cost vs owned numbers, clear level vs progress, consistent typography and alignments across both states. This is an actual high fidelity game UI concept, not wireframes, not phone hardware, not isometric. Generous internal padding, compact content with no huge blank zones, ornate art subordinate to controls. External tiny footer below both frames “界面方案 · 示例数据”.
